# About Trident Photobooths

Photo and video booth rentals from Brandon, Manitoba, for weddings and events across the prairies.

Page: https://www.tridentphotobooths.ca/about

**Trident Photobooths** rents photo and video booths for weddings and events. We are based in Brandon, Manitoba, in the Westman region, and we travel to celebrations across Manitoba, Saskatchewan and Alberta, including Winnipeg, Regina, Saskatoon and Calgary.

Trident Photobooths is part of [Trident Event Group](https://www.tridenteventgroup.ca), a Manitoba event company whose other brands include Trident Music (DJs), Trident Films (videography), Trident Studios (photography) and Roam Content (wedding and event social content). Trident Photobooths is operated by Trident Event Group Inc.

## What we do

We offer three signature booth experiences:

- **Open Air Photobooth** — unlimited prints with custom branded templates, instant digital delivery by SMS or QR code, props, backdrops and an on-site attendant. GIF, boomerang and video options are available.
- **360 Booth** — a slow-motion video platform that sweeps around your guests and captures a cinematic clip, with custom overlays, music and branding, an LED platform and an immediate shareable link.
- **Glam Booth** — studio-grade ring lighting and signature high-key black and white processing, with elegant prints on premium paper.

Packages typically include setup and teardown, an on-site attendant, unlimited sessions, a custom template you approve before the event, instant digital sharing, props and a backdrop.

## Who we work with

We cover weddings, corporate parties, galas, birthday parties, proms, grad nights, holiday events and more. Our hand-selected team, from admin and attendants to production and hosting, is dedicated to making every event run smoothly from setup to teardown.

## Where we go

From our base in Brandon we serve Winnipeg, Regina, Saskatoon and Calgary, plus communities such as Portage la Prairie, Steinbach, Selkirk, Winkler, Morden, Moose Jaw, Dauphin and Swan River. We regularly travel to towns outside Brandon; a travel fee may apply depending on distance.
