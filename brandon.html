Brandon photo booth questions

How much does a photo booth rental cost in Brandon?

Every Brandon booking is quoted for the booth you choose, the number of hours and the date. Send your date, venue and guest count through the inquiry form, or call or text 431-441-2676, and we'll reply with the booth options and packages. Events in towns outside Brandon may include a travel fee depending on distance, and it is shown in the quote.

How much space does the booth need at a Brandon venue?

It depends on the booth and the room. The Open Air and Glam booths fit groups of 2 to 10 or more per session, and the 360 platform holds up to 6 guests at a time. Tell us the venue when you inquire, whether that's a ballroom at the Victoria Inn or a hall at the Keystone Centre, and we'll confirm the floor space and power we need before your date.

Do guests get prints or digital copies?

It depends on the booth. The Open Air booth includes unlimited prints on your custom template plus instant digital copies by text or QR code. The Glam booth produces black and white prints on premium paper. With the 360 booth, each guest gets a shareable link to their slow-motion clip right after their turn, ready to post or send to friends.

Do you offer a 360 booth in Brandon?

Yes. The 360 booth is a slow-motion video platform: an arm sweeps around up to 6 guests while it records, and each clip gets your custom overlays, music and branding. We've run it in Brandon at a Brandon University Bobcats home opener, and it suits birthdays, grad nights, corporate parties and wedding receptions just as well.

Is an attendant included?

Yes. Every package includes a professional on-site attendant who sets up the booth, helps your guests with props and poses, keeps the line moving and handles teardown at the end of the night. For a Brandon wedding or corporate party, that means one less thing for you or your venue staff to manage.
