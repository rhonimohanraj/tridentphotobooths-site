## Service area

We are based in Brandon, Manitoba and serve Winnipeg, Regina, Saskatoon, Calgary and communities across Manitoba, Saskatchewan and Alberta, including Portage la Prairie, Steinbach, Selkirk, Winkler, Morden, Moose Jaw, Dauphin and Swan River. If your venue is a bit off the map, just ask and we will let you know if we can make it work. A travel fee may apply depending on distance.

## What to include

To help us quote accurately, include your event type (wedding, corporate party, gala, birthday, prom, grad night or holiday event), the date, the venue or town, an estimate of your guest count, and whether you are interested in the Open Air Photobooth, the 360 Booth or the Glam Booth. If you would like a custom template with your names, date, logo or event theme, mention it; you will review and approve the design before your event.
