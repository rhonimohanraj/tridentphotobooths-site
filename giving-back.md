# Trident Photobooths Gives Back

> The photo booths we have donated to fundraisers and community events, so every guest leaves with a keepsake from a night that did some good.

## Why we do this: The people you want to see win.

Brandon gave us our first dance floors. Basement parties, high school dances, a first wedding with one speaker and a laptop. Every booking since then started with someone in this community taking a chance on us.

So when Holden's family raises money for A-T research, or United Way fills a room to fund Helping Hands and Samaritan House, we bring what we are good at. Our sound, our booths, our cameras and our crew, at no cost to them.

We believe a community that gives back grows together. The boy at the fundraiser dinner, the volunteer ladling soup at Helping Hands, the shop owner at a Chamber event: these are the people we want to see win. Every year since 2022 we have shown up for them, and when they win, all of us do.

— Rhoni Mohanraj, Founder, Trident Event Group

## What we give: What a booth brings to a good cause.

Everything on this page was given for free: our services, our gear and our crew's time. Cash sponsorships are a separate thing. This page is a record of the work we donated.

- **A staffed booth**: Set up, run and torn down by our attendant, so organizers have one less thing to manage.
- **Prints with the cause on them**: Templates carry the organization's name and logo, so every print and shared photo spreads the word.
- **A place people gather**: A booth draws a crowd, and a crowd in the room is a crowd near the donation table.

## The stories: Booths we brought for free.

### Hope 4 Holden, year two (June 2026)

With [Hope 4 Holden](https://hope4holden.com).

Holden is a local boy living with ataxia-telangiectasia (A-T), a rare condition that affects movement and the immune system. His family turned their fight into a fundraiser dinner and golf tournament for A-T research and the families living with it. When they asked us back for a second year, the answer was easy.

- Trident Photobooths: A digital photo booth for the dinner guests.

### A comedy night for the Women's Centre (March 2026)

With Western Manitoba Women's Centre.

The Western Manitoba Women's Centre supports women, gender-diverse people and their children across the region. For its comedy fundraiser at the Victoria Inn, we donated a photo booth so guests could take a piece of the night home.

- Trident Photobooths: A donated photo booth, set up and run by our attendant, Danny.

### Designer Bag Bingo for Habitat for Humanity (February 2026)

With Habitat for Humanity Brandon.

Habitat for Humanity Brandon builds affordable homes alongside lower-income working families. Its Designer Bag Bingo at the Legion is one of the liveliest fundraisers in town, and we signed on as the entertainment.

- Trident Photobooths: A photo booth for the bingo crowd.

### Women of Westman, five years running (2022 to 2026)

With [Brandon Chamber of Commerce](https://brandonchamber.ca).

The Brandon Chamber of Commerce brings local business owners together, and its yearly conference for women in business, first Superwoman and now Women of Westman, is a day we look forward to. Since 2022 our photo booth has been part of it.

- Trident Photobooths: A photo booth at every conference since 2022.

### EVO Golf Holiday Classic (December 2025)

With [EVO Golf](https://www.evogolf.ca), [CancerCare Manitoba Foundation](https://www.cancercarefdn.mb.ca).

EVO Golf's Holiday Classic raises money for CancerCare Manitoba Foundation, and every dollar raised stays in Manitoba. EVO asked local businesses to donate their time, and four Trident teams said yes.

- Trident Photobooths: A photo booth for players and guests.

### The first Hope 4 Holden (June 2025)

With [Hope 4 Holden](https://hope4holden.com).

The first Hope 4 Holden filled the Grand Salon at the Victoria Inn for a dinner, followed the next day by a golf tournament at Glen Lea. Every dollar went toward A-T research and support for families. We wanted the night to feel as big as the cause.

- Trident Photobooths: A digital photo booth for the dinner guests.

## Nominate a cause: Know someone who deserves a win?

Tell us about the person, the cause or the community event. Each year we choose a handful of causes to support with our services and our time.

- Based in Westman, or in a community we serve across Manitoba and Saskatchewan.
- A registered charity, a non-profit, a school, or a family raising money for someone in need.
- At least six weeks before the event, so we can plan the crew and gear.
- We read every nomination, even in the years we can only say yes to a few.

Email hello@tridenteventgroup.ca with the subject "Trident Gives Back nomination".

To every organizer, volunteer and guest who filled these rooms: thank you for letting us be part of it.
