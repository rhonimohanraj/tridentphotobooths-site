## Our services: three ways to make memories

Each booth experience is crafted to create unforgettable moments for every type of event — from intimate weddings to corporate celebrations.

### Open Air Photobooth

"Where unforgettable memories are made." Our open-air booth offers unlimited prints, custom templates, and a fully branded experience for your event. The classic experience, reimagined: groups big and small, props galore, and instant photo delivery right to your guests' phones.

- Unlimited prints with custom branded templates
- Instant digital delivery via SMS or QR code
- Props, backdrops, and on-site attendant included
- GIF, boomerang, and video options available
- Perfect for weddings, galas, and corporate events

### 360 Booth

"The new immersive experience in 360." Our slow-motion video platform spins around your guests, capturing every angle in a cinematic boomerang clip. All the way around, all the drama: step onto the platform and let the arm sweep around you.

- Slow-motion video in stunning quality
- Immediate shareable link sent to your guests
- Custom overlays, music, and branding
- LED platform with optional lighting effects
- Attendant manages every session

### Glam Booth

"You never go wrong with beautiful black & white." Studio-quality ring lighting with high-key glam filters for a timeless, editorial look. Luxury lighting, timeless results: a polished, magazine-worthy portrait for every guest.

- Professional ring-light setup for flawless skin tones
- Signature high-key black & white processing
- Elegant physical prints on premium paper
- Ideal for weddings, mitzvahs, and upscale galas
- Fully customizable template and branding

## Common questions

**What areas do you serve?**
We serve Winnipeg, Regina, Saskatoon, Calgary, and communities across Manitoba, Saskatchewan, and Alberta — from the big cities to the small towns in between. If you're a bit off the map, just ask and we'll let you know if we can make it work.

**What types of events do you cover?**
We cover all types of events including weddings, corporate parties, galas, birthday parties, proms, grad nights, holiday events, and more. If you're celebrating, we want to be there.

**Is there an attendant at the event?**
Yes — all of our packages include a professional on-site attendant who manages the booth, assists your guests, and makes sure everything runs smoothly from setup to teardown.

**Can you customize the photo templates?**
Absolutely. Every package includes a custom-designed template featuring your names, date, logo, or event theme. You'll review and approve the design before your event.

**Do you travel to smaller towns?**
Yes! We regularly travel to towns outside Brandon. A travel fee may apply depending on distance, but we work hard to keep our pricing accessible. Just inquire with your location and we'll give you an accurate quote.

**What's included in each package?**
Packages typically include setup & teardown, an on-site attendant, unlimited sessions, custom templates, instant digital sharing, props, and a backdrop. Contact us for full package details.

**How many guests can use the booth at once?**
The open-air and glam booths can comfortably fit groups of 2–10+ guests per session. The 360 platform holds up to 6 guests depending on the setup. The more the merrier!
