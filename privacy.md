# Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information.

Page: https://www.tridentphotobooths.ca/privacy

**Effective date:** October 13, 2026

This policy explains how Trident Event Group Inc. ("Trident", "we", "us"), which operates Trident Photobooths, collects, uses and protects personal information through tridentphotobooths.ca. We follow the principles of Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

## Information we collect

- **Inquiry and booking details** you give us through our inquiry form, by email or by phone: your name, email address, phone number, event date, event type, venue or location, guest count, the services you are interested in, and anything else you choose to tell us.
- **Event content** such as photos and videos captured by our booths at your event, and the contact details guests enter to receive their photos by SMS or QR code.
- **Website usage information** collected by analytics tools, such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type. These tools use cookies or similar technologies.

## How we use it

- To answer your inquiry, check availability and prepare a quote.
- To plan, confirm and deliver your booking, including custom templates and photo delivery to you and your guests.
- To send booking-related messages such as confirmations, invoices and event reminders.
- To understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Cookies and analytics

Our website uses analytics tools that set cookies to measure traffic and usage in aggregate. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

## Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers we need to run our business and deliver your event, such as our booking/CRM software, payment processors, email and messaging providers, photo delivery and hosting services, and analytics tools. These providers may store information outside Canada and are expected to protect it. We may also disclose information where required by law.

## Retention

We keep inquiry and booking records for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements, and then delete or anonymize them.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or withdraw your consent to marketing messages at any time. Send requests to [hello@tridenteventgroup.ca](mailto:hello@tridenteventgroup.ca) and we will respond within a reasonable time.
