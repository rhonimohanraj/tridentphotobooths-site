## Winnipeg photo booth questions

**How much does a photo booth rental cost in Winnipeg?**
Winnipeg events are quoted individually, based on the booth, the number of hours and your date. Send the date, the venue and a rough guest count through the inquiry form, or call or text 431-441-2676, and we'll come back with booth options and packages. For towns outside the city, such as Selkirk or Steinbach, a travel fee may apply depending on distance.

**How much space does the booth need at a Winnipeg venue?**
Winnipeg rooms vary a lot, so we check yours before the day. The Open Air and Glam booths handle groups of 2 to 10 or more at a time, and the 360 platform holds up to 6 guests. Let us know the venue and room, whether it's a hotel ballroom downtown or a community centre in the suburbs, and we'll confirm the floor space and power the booth needs.

**Do guests get prints or digital copies?**
That depends on the booth you pick. Guests at the Open Air booth leave with unlimited prints on your custom template and can grab digital copies straight away by text or QR code. The Glam booth prints black and white portraits on premium paper. At the 360 booth, guests receive a shareable link to their slow-motion clip moments after stepping off the platform.

**Do you offer a 360 booth in Winnipeg?**
Yes, the 360 booth comes to Winnipeg events. Up to 6 guests step onto the LED platform while the arm sweeps around them and records a slow-motion clip with your overlays, music and branding. It works well for corporate parties, product launches, grad nights and wedding receptions, and every clip arrives as a shareable link.

**Is an attendant included?**
Every Winnipeg package includes a professional on-site attendant. They set up before guests arrive, run the booth all night, help people with props and poses, and pack everything up at the end, so your planner and the venue staff can focus on the rest of the event.
